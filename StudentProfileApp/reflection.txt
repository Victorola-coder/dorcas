Reflection (Lab 3)

Building the Student Profile app showed me how one screen can collect several
kinds of input - text fields, and a RadioGroup for a single choice - and bundle
them all into a single Intent using multiple putExtra calls. Adding validation
made me think about the user's mistakes before they happen, so the app now
refuses to move forward until every field is filled and a gender is chosen.
Wrapping the fields in Material TextInputLayouts and showing the result inside a
MaterialCardView also taught me how much cleaner an app feels with proper
Material Design components instead of plain views.
