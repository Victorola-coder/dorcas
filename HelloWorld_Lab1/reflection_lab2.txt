Reflection (Lab 2)

This lab showed me how an Intent acts as the bridge between two activities:
MainActivity builds an explicit Intent for SecondActivity and attaches data with
putExtra, while SecondActivity reads it back with intent.getStringExtra. Adding
the EditText made the navigation feel real, since the second screen now shows
whatever the user actually types instead of a fixed string. The trickiest part
was remembering to register SecondActivity in AndroidManifest.xml, because the
app compiles fine without it but crashes the moment you try to open the screen.
